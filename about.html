About

Hi, I'm Harry.

I'm Harry Hart, a Product Design student at the University of Canterbury who designs digital products for the health, fitness and nutrition world. I combine human-centred research with high-fidelity visual design to build apps and websites that make healthy habits feel simple, not overwhelming.

Alongside my degree, I'm working toward certification as a personal trainer and nutritionist and coaching beta clients, building the brand and digital tools for my own online coaching business from the ground up.
